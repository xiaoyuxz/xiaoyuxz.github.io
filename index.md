I am a first-year PhD student at the [Center for Language and Cognition (CLCG)](https://www.rug.nl/research/clcg/), University of Groningen, supervised by [Dr. Federico Pianzola](https://federicopianzola.me/portfolio/) and [Dr. Janina Wildfeuer](http://janinawildfeuer.de/index.html).

My research focuses on empirical studies of multimodal narratives, currently using [webtoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webtoon) to investigate computational approaches to narrative analysis in the infinite canvas format. I am also interested in applying natural language processing and computer vision to broader research on multimodal communication and the humanities. Part of my work contributes to the [GOLEM Project](https://golemlab.eu/).

Previously, I completed a BA in Translation Studies (2022) and an MA in Digital Humanities (2024), during which I contributed to the [TINTIN Project](https://www.visuallanguagelab.com/tintin) by conducting sentiment analysis on comics.

Collaborations, feedback, or chats are all welcome. Feel free to email me: [xiaoyu.zhou@rug.nl](mailto:xiaoyu.zhou@rug.nl)
